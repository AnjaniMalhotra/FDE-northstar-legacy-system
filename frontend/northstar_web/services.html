Enterprise Capabilities

Everything between booking and payment

We don't own trucks — we manage carrier relationships, continuous safety vetting, dock tracking, and invoice audit so shipments move and get paid without surprises.

Truckload Freight Brokerage

We book your load with the right carrier on our network at a fair, agreed rate — eliminating spot-market guesswork.

  • Rate confirmation locked in at booking
  • Carrier matched by lane, capacity, and performance history
  • Pickup and delivery appointments tracked end-to-end

Carrier Vetting & Management

Every carrier on our network is screened before they haul a single load, and continuously monitored while active.

  • FMCSA safety ratings and insurance certificates checked daily
  • Historic billing errors and detention dispute counts tracked per carrier
  • Elevated Risk carriers reviewed individually on every invoice

Freight Audit & Payment Settlement

Every invoice — base linehaul, fuel surcharge, detention — is cross-checked against agreed terms and dock timestamps before authorization.

  • Detention charges verified against real RFID/Optical gate timestamps
  • Disputed charges placed on hold with clear audit notes
  • Documented human approval on every invoice

Real-Time Shipment Tracking

Log into your shipper portal to see exactly where a load stands from booking request to final delivery.

  • Live status milestones: Scheduled, Enroute, At Dock, Delivered
  • Complete history of rate approvals and delivery logs in one place
  • No phone calls or manual check-calls required
