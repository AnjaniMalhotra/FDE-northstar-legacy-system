Our Story

Building transparency into truckload freight

Founded in 2004, Northstar Freight connects shippers with vetted carriers across 10 major U.S. commercial logistics hub markets — with rigorous audit controls built into every load.

Carrier Quality First

Vetted, not just matched

We don't post loads to unvetted spot boards and hope for the best. Every carrier on our network undergoes strict safety verification, active insurance audits, and historic performance monitoring before hauling their first shipment.

Audit & Compliance Discipline

Eliminating freight billing overcharges

Industry research shows 5% to 8% of freight invoices contain detention or linehaul rate errors. At Northstar, every invoice is cross-referenced with real dock gate timestamps and contracted booking terms before payment release.
